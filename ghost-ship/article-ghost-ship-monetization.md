# How to Build a 15-Second Minecraft Ghost Ship That Earns

*A production and monetization playbook for creators.*

Short voxel clips are one of the cheapest visual formats to produce and one of the easiest to repurpose. A 15-second ghost ship can be built in an afternoon, costs nothing in software, and fits every major short-form platform without modification. This guide covers the exact pipeline and the revenue paths that follow.

## 1. Why this format works

- **Instant recognition.** Voxel art needs no explanation. Viewers decode it in under a second.
- **Loopable and short.** 15 seconds fits X, Reels, TikTok and Shorts, and short clips lift completion rate, which platforms reward.
- **Mood sells.** A single strong atmosphere (cold moonlight, fog, glowing cyan) beats a complicated scene.
- **Reusable assets.** One ship becomes a series: ghost ship, ghost castle, ghost train.

## 2. The production pipeline

### Step 1: Pick one idea and one palette
Ghost ship: dark oak hull, translucent sails, cyan soul-fire, navy night. Limit yourself to 3 to 4 colors. Restraint reads as professional.

### Step 2: Build the scene
Two routes, both free:

- **In Minecraft:** Build the ship in Creative mode with a shader pack (BSL or Complementary) and a cinematic camera mod (Replay Mod or Flashback). Record at 1080p/60.
- **Programmatic voxels:** Generate the ship in code (three.js or Blender) and render frames deterministically. This is how the sample clip was made, and the source is in the `source/` folder. The advantage is exact control and perfect reproducibility.

### Step 3: Direct the camera
Fifteen seconds allows one move. Use a slow orbit that ends on a wider reveal. Start low to make the ship feel large, finish higher to show the whole silhouette.

### Step 4: Add atmosphere
Four ingredients carry the look:

1. Exponential fog for depth.
2. Emissive lanterns with flicker.
3. Floating particles (soul wisps).
4. A bright moon block as the key light and visual anchor.

### Step 5: Sound design
Half the impact is audio. Layer a low drone, brown-noise wind, a faint high bell, and water texture. Fade in over 1.5s and out over 1.5s so loops feel clean. Sound-on posts get more saves and shares.

### Step 6: Export
- 1280x720 or 1920x1080, H.264, AAC audio, 30fps, under 20MB.
- Also export 9:16 (1080x1920) for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- Add a 2-word title in the last 5 seconds. Keep it big and short.

## 3. Packaging for engagement

- **Hook in the first second.** No black intro. Movement and mood from frame one (a 1s fade is the upper limit).
- **One question per post.** "Board or turn back?" gives people an easy reply.
- **Link in the first reply**, not the post.
- **Thumbnail/cover:** pick the frame with the moon and the lit sails.
- **Post natively** to each platform. Cross-posted watermarks cut reach.

## 4. Monetization by platform

| Platform | How it pays | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **YouTube Shorts** | Ad revenue share via the Partner Programme | Needs 1,000 subscribers plus 10M Shorts views in 90 days, or standard thresholds. Pin a link to a long-form build video. |
| **TikTok** | Creator Rewards Program (videos over 1 minute), LIVE gifts, TikTok Shop | Shorts under 1 min do not pay directly. Use them to funnel to longer videos and products. |
| **Instagram Reels** | Bonuses (invite-based), subscriptions, brand deals | Strong for brand and studio partnerships. |
| **X (Twitter)** | Ad revenue share for Premium creators, subscriptions | Pays on verified-user engagement. Quote-reply threads boost it. |
| **Facebook** | Content Monetization programme, Reels bonuses | Older audiences share atmospheric content heavily. |
| **Patreon / Ko-fi** | Direct fan support | Offer world downloads, schematics, 4K files. |

Rules and thresholds change often, so verify current terms on each platform before you plan around them.

## 5. Income that does not depend on a platform

Platform payouts are volatile. Build owned revenue:

1. **Sell the world and schematics.** A downloadable ghost ship (.schem or .mcworld) at $3 to $9 on Gumroad, Itch.io or Patreon.
2. **License loops to other creators.** Sell royalty-free 4K loops and background packs on Pond5, Envato or your own store. Moody fantasy loops sell steadily for stream overlays and music visualizers.
3. **Commissions.** Offer custom builds and trailers for Minecraft servers. Typical range: $50 to $500 per piece.
4. **Music channels.** Lofi, dark ambient and gaming channels need looping visuals. Pitch a pack.
5. **Brand and server deals.** Servers and launchers pay for trailers and cinematic builds.
6. **Print-on-demand.** Cover frames as posters and shirts, with clear attribution to Minecraft-compatible, non-infringing designs.

## 6. Compliance, briefly

- Follow Mojang's Commercial Usage Guidelines: you may monetize videos of gameplay and builds, but you may not imply an official relationship or sell Minecraft assets as your own.
- Do not use copyrighted music. Synthesize your own or use licensed libraries.
- Use "Minecraft-style" in product titles unless you are clearly selling content for the game under the guidelines.

## 7. The 30-day plan

| Week | Goal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Publish 1 hero clip (this ghost ship). Post natively on 4 platforms. |
| 2 | Release 3 variants: day version, storm version, ship-in-a-bottle. Test hooks. |
| 3 | Launch the downloadable world and open a Patreon or Ko-fi. |
| 4 | Package 10 loops as a pack. Pitch 20 creators and 5 servers. |

## 8. Metrics to watch

- **Completion rate** above 70% means the clip is working.
- **Saves and shares per view**: the strongest signal for reach.
- **Reply rate** to your question hook.
- **Click-through** from reply link to article or store.

## Takeaway

One atmospheric idea, one clean camera move, strong sound, and disciplined packaging. Then turn attention into owned assets: worlds, loops, commissions. The clip is the advertisement, and the catalogue is the business.
